## What was made

A new page, `/camera/` (`camera/`, described in [camera/README.md](README.md)):

- Live camera and photo editor running **every World Look** (all 19, from `johansson-town/src/render/looks.js`,
  imported, not copied), alone or **mixed up to three deep** with per-layer intensity; a dice for a surprise mix.
