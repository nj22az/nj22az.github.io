# Pocket Camera — 29 October 2026

The owner: "I want you make a fun mini app for the office of Johansson website , photo editing app and a live camera app ,
I want to be able to use all the shaders from Johansson world on my photos maybe even a combination. Make it feel like a
polished camera app playful like Gameboy camera , that said I don't want to have a Gameboy on screen: use Apple design",
and during the work: "Bring the die cut stickers to the camera app".

## What was made

A new page, `/camera/` (`camera/`, described in [camera/README.md](README.md)):

- Live camera and photo editor running **every World Look** (all 19, from `johansson-town/src/render/looks.js`,
  imported, not copied), alone or **mixed up to three deep** with per-layer intensity; a dice for a surprise mix.
- Self-timer, shape (4:3, 1:1, 16:9), zoom, date stamp in the hotel photos' style, flip camera, shutter sound and
  flash. Editor: Looks, Adjust (Light, Contrast, Colour, Warmth, Pattern), Stickers, hold for the original.
- **Die-cut stickers** from the studio (`nj22az/jojo` `art/stickers/`: Thuan 26, Fuji 28, Thuan & Nils 8), on the
  viewfinder or a photo: drag, pinch and turn, corner handle, flip, delete; kept in the recipe and re-editable.
- On-device album (IndexedDB): photo, untouched source, thumbnail and recipe; Edit re-opens from the source; Share or
  Download; Delete with an action sheet.
- Apple camera/Photos design (always dark, system font, the camera's yellow, translucent material); a wide window puts
  the controls beside the picture. World's own Look swatches stand in for the live thumbnails until the camera starts.
- Homepage: a "Pocket Camera" project card (`CONFIG.projectExplorer`, `project-explorer.js` preview `camera`, card
  images `camera/card.webp` and `card-narrow.webp`), the noscript list and `sitemap.xml`.

Engine notes (details in the README): one string replacement in World's shader source, `unit()` following the picture's
height so previews match saves; a first pass of our own (crop, mirror, adjustments, edge-keeping smoothing for line
Looks, exposure for the ink Looks); phones use World's light versions in the viewfinder only; saves are full quality,
tiled, up to 4096 px / 16 MP.

## Design audit and second pass (29 October 2026, later)

The owner: "Audit yourself what's wrong pls fix it, be designer for Gameboy camera and DS camera and a comic Artist",
then "I want to be able to use my own photos too". Findings and what was done:

| As | Finding | Done |
|---|---|---|
| Game Boy Camera designer | Every hand-held photo was printed in a frame; ours had none | `frames.js`: Print, Panel, Film, in the viewfinder and the save alike |
| Game Boy Camera designer | Brightness was set live on the pad; ours only in the editor | Drag up or down on the viewfinder for light, a sun on the edge, a chip to put it back |
| Game Boy Camera / DSi designer | No trick lens (the mirror shots) | Mirror: Twin and Quad, before the Looks |
| Game Boy Camera / DSi designer | The first screen and the empty album had no one in them | Fuji or Thuan waves on the first screen; Fuji salutes the empty album |
| DSi designer | Small text chips crowded the top; settings needed room for more | iOS settings row behind a chevron; what is on shows beside it in yellow |
| DSi designer | The dice rolled any pair, some of it noise (Old TV under Print: moiré rings) | 16 named mixes chosen by eye on a photo and on the town; 8 rejected pairs recorded |
| DSi designer | Most taps were silent | A soft tick for taps, a pop for stickers; Sound in the settings mutes them |
| Comic artist | No lettering | Speech, Shout, Thought, Caption with the user's words, in Baloo 2 like the stickers |
| Comic artist | No panel border | The Panel frame, which balloons and stickers may cross |
| Comic artist | Busy photos ink every grain; no control | Adjust: Detail (fewer, bolder lines or more) |
| Designer, found while testing | The camera's intensity slider covered the light chip at the foot of the picture | It stands in for the strip at the strip's height (beside it in a wide window) |
| Designer, found while testing | A toast repeated the setting the row already showed, over the row | Settings changes no longer toast |
| Owner | "Use my own photos too": the editor's import was a small icon | **Photos** at the top right; several photos at once go into the album in the camera's Look; drop or paste |
| Owner (stickers) | Own pictures as stickers | Sheet **Mine**: any photo becomes a die-cut sticker; a lifted subject (Copy in Photos, then Paste) is cut round its outline |

Considered and left: inking the stickers into the Looks (they stay die-cut stickers on the print; balloons stay on top
as a letterer's work does); Pocket palettes (green, grey, rose) would need a second replacement in World's shader.

Checks after the second pass: `node camera/tests/looks-gl.test.mjs` passes (with frame, trick, Detail and balloon
fields); `node camera/tests/camera.browser.mjs` **36/36** at phone and desktop size (new: settings row, the dice's
named mix, light dragged and reset, a lettered balloon, the print border in the saved file, Frame tab with Film and
Twin, Detail, two photos imported at once, own stickers from a cut-out and from a photo, deleting one, both kept
after a reload). Balloons, frames and the 24 candidate mixes were also rendered and judged on their own sheets.

## Limits and next steps

- **Not checked on real devices**: iPhone Safari and Android Chrome (camera permissions, front-camera mirroring,
  the share sheet's "Save Image", frame rate with the heavy Looks, memory on a 12 MP save, the camera coming back
  after the system takes the graphics away). Do that first; the frame-rate governor and the light shader versions are
  there for phones but were not measured on one.
- The Pattern adjustment changes dots, strokes and lines; Pocket, 16-bit and Old TV size their pixels and scanlines
  from the picture itself in World's code, so Pattern does not change them.
- The Thuan stickers are the 19 October sheets with the cartoon Thuan, drawn before her Pixar body replaced it in
  World; Pixar stickers would be made in the studio first and then `tools/build-camera-stickers.py` re-run.
- Not built: video recording. A possible next step (MediaRecorder on the canvas).
- "Copy Subject" and Paste rely on the browser's clipboard (`navigator.clipboard.read`, asked for on Safari); not tried
  on a real iPhone. A lifted subject saved as a file and chosen with "From a photo" is cut the same way.
