# Scandic parking audit — 25 October 2026

For field service engineers: free guest parking is preferred. Paid parking is
Easy only when both payment at the hotel and inclusion on the hotel receipt are confirmed. Paid external or unclear payment
is Avoid. Unknown payment is not evidence that hotel payment is impossible; unknown
receipt inclusion stays unknown. No hotel-quality ratings are implied.
