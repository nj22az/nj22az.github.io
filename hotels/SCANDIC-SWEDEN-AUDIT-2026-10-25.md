# Swedish Scandic parking audit — 25 October 2026

For field service engineers seeking practical parking without extra receipts.
**91 Swedish property pages reviewed: 72 Avoid, 19 with a free parking option.**
This covers the Swedish links surfaced in Scandic’s official destination catalogue,
including Scandic Go, Haymarket and Downtown Camper. It is not an audit of every
hotel chain in Sweden or a guarantee about future catalogue changes.

## Decision rule

Paid parking qualifies as Easy only when both payment at the hotel and inclusion
on the hotel receipt are confirmed. An unknown fact stays unknown and makes the
paid option Avoid; it is not proof that reception cannot help. No paid Swedish
entry meets both confirmations in the reviewed information. Free usable parking,
including nearby public spaces, remains green unless an attributed access warning
applies. This is a parking/admin preference, not a hotel quality or safety rating.

## Arrival and payment conditions that matter

- **Scandic Kista:** buying a permit at reception establishes hotel payment, but
  says nothing conclusive about inclusion on the hotel receipt. It remains Avoid.
- **Haymarket:** obtain the garage entry ticket as instructed and ask reception for
  the discount. A reception discount is not confirmation of hotel payment or billing.
- **Scandic Europa:** the hotel allocation is on floor 6 of Nordstan; other floors
  incur the ordinary garage tariff. Booking through the hotel does not prove a
  combined hotel receipt.
- **Scandic S:t Jörgen:** the hotel reports renovation restrictions preventing cars
  and taxis from stopping at the entrance; it recommends the garage for direct
  reception access. Weekday and weekend parking tariffs differ.
- **Scandic Kiruna:** the parking page still links construction-related temporary
  driving directions, including an older dated attachment. Confirm the current
  route; the garage’s stated 06:00–00:00 tariff does not establish free night parking.
- **Scandic Gamla Stan:** no hotel parking, restricted vehicle approach hours and
  few nearby spaces. Riddarholmen has a daily charge; one garage suggestion is two
  underground stops away. No numeric daily fee is invented.
- **Scandic No 25:** the hotel refers guests to Burggrevegatan, about 150 m away.
  [Parkering Göteborg](https://www.parkeringgoteborg.se/vara-anlaggningar/p-hus-burggrevegatan/)
  confirms the garage charge: SEK 26/hour 08:00–22:00, SEK 2/hour otherwise, daily;
  a payment machine is available. The operator’s gated permit spaces require a key.
- **Scandic Hallandia:** its garage entry contains a 1 June–1 October closure note;
  this is a seasonal condition, not a claim that it is closed on the audit date.
- **Scandic Elmia:** the original personal SEK 105 parking payment and separate
  receipt remain intact. Its official page now lists SEK 10/hour; the current
  tariff is recorded in this audit and does not replace that personal payment.
