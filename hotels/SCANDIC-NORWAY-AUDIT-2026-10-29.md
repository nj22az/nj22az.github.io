# Norwegian Scandic parking audit — 29 October 2026

Reviewed all **81** Norwegian property entries in Scandic’s [current national catalogue](https://www.scandichotels.com/en/destinations/norway), including partner and “by Scandic” properties. This is complete review coverage of that snapshot, not confirmation of every billing or access condition. Other chains are outside scope.

**Before:** only Flesland Airport, Byporten and Ørnen were registered; 78 properties remained unreviewed. **Now:** 75 added, those three refreshed, and three charge-unknown properties withheld. Norwegian Scandic register: **78 hotels: 18 Green, 1 Blue, 0 Yellow, 59 Red**. All 78 have named official hotel coordinates.

Full directory: **211 hotels: 57 Green, 4 Blue, 1 Yellow, 149 Red; all mapped**. The other three Blue entries are existing registration requirements at Hotel Marina, Scandic Kolding and Scandic Västerås; their parking evidence dates have not been reset by this audit.

## Rules and evidence

- Green: a confirmed free guest option on weekdays and weekends. Known capacity and walking distances are disclosed; a space is not guaranteed.
- Blue: the free option requires vehicle registration. Sunnfjord requires the inPark reception tablet. Other free properties do not state registration requirements; this remains unknown, not “no registration”.
- Yellow requires both hotel payment and inclusion on the hotel receipt. No Norwegian property reviewed confirms the latter; reception payment, an operator machine in reception, or a reception discount alone does not qualify.
- Red: confirmed paid parking without both billing facts, or difficult access. Unknown payment routes remain null, not asserted external. A named external operator alone does not establish how payment is made.
- Unknown charges remain outside the directory, CSV and map. “At a cost” confirms paid status but does not confirm an amount. No free overnight period is inferred outside published tariff windows.
- Tariffs are published current information, not prices paid on a stay. Room prices, ratings and personal experiences have not been invented.
- Property identity/address checked against each hotel’s own Hotel structured data. Named hotel coordinates come from the official catalogue, with property id/name retained; previous three map points are preserved. No city centres or viewport centres used.
- [Structured evidence](SCANDIC-NORWAY-EVIDENCE-2026-10-29.json) retains each official source URL, check date, SHA-256, weekday/weekend tariffs, registration/payment/receipt states, capacities, distances and unresolved facts. Eight linked official parking subpages were also read.
