Installation & adding the game to Steam

On Steam Deck, switch to Desktop Mode and open Konsole. Paste:

curl -fL --progress-bar https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nj22az/nj22az.github.io/main/johansson-world/install.sh -o /tmp/johansson-world-install.sh &&
sh /tmp/johansson-world-install.sh

The installer verifies the download and installs in your user account. No compilation, sudo or SteamOS system changes.

  1. Open Steam → Add a Game → Add a Non-Steam Game.
  2. Select Johansson World, or browse to ~/.local/bin/johansson-world.
  3. Leave forced Proton compatibility disabled and select a Gamepad controller layout.
  4. Return to Gaming Mode and launch.

Development record

Change log

Implementation, release and proposals are recorded separately.

18 October 2026Published preview · 0.2.4

An island guide with character.

Large illustrated icons replace the long pause-menu list. Island, People and Settings have shoulder-button navigation; resident portraits and short labels make destinations easier to recognise. A white, indigo and coral palette carries through the title, inventory and dialogue.

The new preset puts run on R2, looking closely on L2 and jumping on X. Existing custom assignments are preserved. Controller symbols follow reported Steam Deck, Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo devices; unknown face buttons show all four positions with the required one highlighted. A manual override handles devices that report themselves differently.

Read the UI audit and reference research → · Implementation and checks →

The reference study covers Animal Crossing, Tomodachi Life and Shenmue. Original repo SVGs carry semantic SF Symbol names for future Apple ports; Linux includes no Apple glyph data. Automated controller, four-resolution layout and native startup/save checks pass. Physical Steam Deck and real controller testing remain pending.

18 October 2026Both editions

Let the night sound like the night.

The gecko beeps and repeating cricket tone are retired. Soft, irregular filtered-noise calls replace the electronic wildlife sounds, while waves, rain and ferry signals remain. The web game receives this audio fix and keeps its existing interface.

17 October 2026Published preview · 0.2.3

Ready to try on Linux.

The new controls and resident-life systems are now in the download. Full action labels wrap without truncation, captions stay clear of action buttons, and the repeating high-pitched night cricket loop is omitted in Linux.

Download 0.2.3 and read the release notes →

Automated controller, handheld layout, gameplay and native Linux checks passed. Physical Steam Deck performance remains unverified.

17 October 2026Both editions

Keep the town, retire two minigames.

The cycling mode and Thuan’s storage-room minigame are removed. Their prompts and navigation are removed, the storage game’s separate assets are retired, and old storage links return to Johansson World. Normal shopping, resident routines and decorative bicycles remain.

A smaller download is expected where those assets were bundled. No web startup-speed improvement is claimed without measurement.

17 October 2026Linux preview · 0.2.3

Controls that feel at home on a console.

A dedicated Start menu, first-play instructions, custom buttons and stick axes, sensitivity, dead zone, invert Y and camera settings. Controller loss pauses the game. Hiding the HUD keeps the menu accessible.

17 October 2026Linux preview · 0.2.3

Everyone has a life to step into.

A resident selector, individual saved positions and owned beds at existing home addresses. Families and housemates keep their shared addresses. Night workers follow their own sleep times. Repeated insults can provoke a rare quarrel; everyday conversation stays peaceful.

17 October 2026Proposal

A smaller Minato, a wider sea.

Reduce empty housing and the oversized northern land mass. Keep the rural fishing village, rebuild roads around one connected plan, and give the ferry useful islands to visit.

Next world pass / Linux only

Less empty land.
More island life.

The current registry has 31 named residents and 17 vacant home addresses. Build the island around households and useful journeys.

01

One coherent road plan

A clear town spine, aligned cross streets and shared back lanes. Replace redundant loops around individual buildings with short door approaches.

02

Homes with people in them

Preserve families and housemates. Give every resident a real address and bed. Turn surplus housing into gardens, shared spaces or shoreline.

03

Keep the fishing village

A small inhabited Hoshizaki, with its pier, boats and working households, a short rural journey from town.

04

Let the ferry matter

Keep Kitano-jima useful. Explore a farming island and a later outer island, each with activities, residents, an accessible berth and a reliable way home.

These are design proposals. Coastline, roads, terrain, home entrances and walking routes need to move together before this can be a playable change.
