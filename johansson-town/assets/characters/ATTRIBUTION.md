# Current resident assets

The ten current residents use the five local Quaternius low-poly bases in `residents/`, with individual colours, accessories and independent animation. Their original creator notices are in `residents/PROVENANCE.md`, `LICENSE-MEN.txt` and `LICENSE-WOMEN.txt`.

Thuan keeps the female casual base with a fitted ribbon and an original skinned expression mesh. The former soft Thuan model, `realistic/yuri-playful.glb`, is retained only as a static Sakura collectible; its source and licence records remain in `realistic/`.

The unused MakeHuman, living-cast, VRoid and alternative realistic model files were removed from published assets in September 2026. Historical notices and source receipts remain in Git history. These prototypes no longer supply residents or fallback appearances.

## Johansson (player, third-person view)

`johansson/johansson.glb` is built from CC0 MakeHuman assets (base mesh, targets, 163-bone default skeleton and weights, skin, eyes, eyebrows, and the shirt, jeans and shoes first fitted for Kenji) with MPFB as an authoring tool only. Shaping, painted skin and hair, the fringe shell, the kariyushi print and all 31 actions are original. Details and rebuild steps: `art/characters/johansson/README.md`.
