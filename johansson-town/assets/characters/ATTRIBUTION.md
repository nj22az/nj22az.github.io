# Character asset archive

The current Johansson Town runtime no longer uses the Kenney Mini Character models. The active residents and player are locally generated human-proportioned Three.js rigs defined in `characters.js`, with individual body proportions, clothing, hair, faces and procedural animation.

The older Kenney Mini Characters are retained only as a legacy CC0 asset archive and are not loaded by the v11 game.

Legacy source: https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters
Legacy license: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Creator: Kenney.

Archived models: character-male-a, character-female-e, character-female-f. The original pack licence remains included as `LICENSE-KENNEY.txt`.

Future higher-detail character work is being evaluated against CC0 Quaternius human bases and animation libraries, but those external models are not currently required for the town to run.
