# Sakura supplier artwork - 30 October 2026

Seven image adverts were generated through the owner-selected Artlist service as part of an approved nine-image batch. They use the existing fictional brands in `src/world/interiors/sakura-trade-posters.js`. The originals are 1728 x 2304 PNGs; these WebP conversions preserve the complete 3:4 artwork. The `-small` copies are 360 x 480, for the wall atlas only. Full-size copies are fetched only for inspection/download.

The B2 wall slot contains the 3:4 artwork with narrow cream paper margins; text is never stretched or cropped. Existing push pins, positions, meshes and collisions are retained.

Two generated proofs are deliberately absent: SHIMAKAZE had duplicated/extra Japanese glyphs, and MINATO-TSURU had a duplicated kana in its footer. Their correct existing canvas designs remain in the game and in the reader until revised artwork is approved. No additional generation was purchased.

Proposed allocation in the supplied artwork brief: office - KITANO 12, UMINEKO gift, ASAGAO rouge; hallway - UMINEKO Draft, Black, SHIMAKAZE; storage - MINATO-TSURU, NAMI SOUR, ASAGAO UV. This change updates existing stair/hall slots only. The closed upper stores have not been opened or decorated by an invented interior.
