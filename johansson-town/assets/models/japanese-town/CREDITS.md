# Japanese Town

By [Nazareno_rojas](https://sketchfab.com/Nazareno_rojas).
[Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/japanese-town-6f58f3bdef404321b8850aab870e110f), supplied by the user.
Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Adaptation for Johansson Town: extracted hipped and gabled timber buildings; removed adjoining landscape, canal, cables and loose props; flattened transforms; grounded and oriented modules; created residential variants without low projecting eaves and lamps; consolidated architecture into one shared material; resized its colour, surface and normal atlases to 1024 pixels and encoded them as JPEG. Source UV coordinates and authored architectural details retained. Shopfront signs, doors and home nameplates are provided by the game.

Rebuild with `node tools/pack-japanese-town.mjs /path/to/japanese_town.glb` from the game directory. Requires Node and Python with Pillow. See `manifest.json` for geometry and payload counts.
