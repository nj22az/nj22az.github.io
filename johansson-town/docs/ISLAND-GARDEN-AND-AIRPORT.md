# Playable island, garden, airport district and Rainflower Lane

This change extends the fictional 1997 island while keeping its warm Okinawan identity. Mount Aoba, a coastal road, the Hoshizaki fishing village and a lighthouse provide a larger island to explore. Walking heights and rendered mountain triangles share one terrain plan.

## Aoba Traditional Garden

Find the garden at the western mountain foothills, through the stone torii at (-28, 108). The old pond and Umi-no-yu have moved here; the bathhouse retains its saved ID, opening hours, admission and interior. A planted stone circuit surrounds the koi pond. A level wooden bridge crosses it; a small shrine, lanterns, bamboo and benches create places to stop. The pond itself blocks walking. Follow the coastal road north from the western quarter or mark the garden in the town directory.

The user's garden photograph guides the gate, planting, paths and shrine composition. All added garden geometry is original. BaderTim/japanese-garden was inspected as a reference for its object list (torii, bridge, koi, lanterns, bamboo and stones) and day-cycle concept. It is a Unity/Blender student project; its repository supplied no reuse licence when inspected on 2 October 2026. No code, models or textures from it are distributed here. Source: https://github.com/BaderTim/japanese-garden

## Kitano-jima airport: its own district under construction

The new passenger district covers about 232 by 112 game metres, beside the existing runway. It contains an arrival plaza, two walkable terminal concourses, eight gate positions and eight working shop interactions: Island Books, Blue Strait Tea, Noodle Kitchen, Seaside Postcards, Travel Essentials, Island Crafts, Music & Cassettes and Departure Coffee. Purchases deduct money, add items and file receipts with linked ledger postings in the Community Hall archive.

An outside sign reads **KITANO-JIMA AIRPORT · UNDER CONSTRUCTION**. The third terminal is visibly framed behind barriers, with a crane; its ground is closed to pedestrians. An airport hotel plot and additional routes are reserved for later phases. These gates and hotel are not operating services yet. The public terminals are open-air concourses with roofed edges; shop visits currently use counters and menus rather than individual room interiors.

Board the ferry from the ticket point at the town quay. A ¥400 return ticket pays for both crossings. The existing airport counter sells the ¥800 Naha commuter return trip. Check in at least ten minutes before departure, wait if necessary, board the animated flight, enter the domestic arrivals room and take the return flight. Rain suspends flights without consuming tickets; missed check-in moves the booking to the next departure without a new charge. Reloading resumes at the last safe shore or destination room rather than over the sea. The runway and sewage works remain closed to walkers.

## Rainflower Lane and Thuan's wardrobe

Mark Rainflower Shopping Lane in the directory, or follow its short connection off the eastern coastal road. Walk along its central street between weathered two-storey shop-houses, upper windows, overhead wires, a flower stand and a red drinks machine. Rain darkens and smooths the roadway material. Signs are English; its architecture is an original interpretation of the user's Shenmue screenshot, without imported game assets.

The wardrobe interaction beside the tailor selects Thuan's usual outfit or her Nozomi-inspired alternative: cream long sleeves, red plaid skirt and brown boots. Selection persists in the save. Her face, hairstyle, skeleton and movement remain hers; swimwear temporarily takes precedence and the selected clothes return afterwards. This supplies an alternate outfit for Thuan, not another character.

## Town development and audit trail

The quay project board spends the town treasury only after service prerequisites are met. The dock radio service unlocks airport cargo shelter works; an outbound flight unlocks the coastal bus; two ferry arrivals unlock the village guesthouse and an additional flight. Each completed project files minutes, works order, completion/delivery, invoice, receipt and ledger entry, linked for the existing one-year archive and audit system. Duplicate completion cannot spend twice.

Haru, Mina and Jun have distinct avatar recipes and biographies, with work, lunch and off-duty phases in the village and airport. Further visitor demand, cargo simulation, terminal routes, shop room interiors and a hotel remain future work; the construction district makes that unfinished growth visible in the game.
