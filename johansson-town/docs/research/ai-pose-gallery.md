# AI Pose Gallery (zh) — which poses suit Johansson World

Research date: 2026-10-28. Source: a shallow clone of <https://github.com/anhao/ai-pose-gallery-zh>, a Chinese mirror of
ギア's (@giaaibusi) *AI Pose Gallery* (<https://ai-pose-gallery.pages.dev/>): 288 poses, 1,439 line drawings of a
featureless mannequin, each pose in up to five views (front, three-quarter, side, high, low).

## Rules for using it

- **Reference only, nothing copied.** The repo's LICENSE says the images and `pose.json`/`meta.json` belong to the
  original author and may not be used commercially; only its gallery code is MIT. No image, trace or data file goes into
  this repo, the Studio or a comic. Like New Yokosuka: study the joint angles, then author our own keyframes in
  `src/avatars/` for our own cast.
- **The mannequin is not our body.** Every figure is a nude female mannequin with exaggerated proportions, often posed
  to show the figure off. Copy where the joints go, never the hips, chest or camera framing. Re-time each pose for the
  resident who does it (Johansson, Mr Fujita, Grandmother Higa and a child do not sit the same way).
- **Story first.** A pose earns a place only if it gives the storyteller a moment: someone waiting, napping, being
  embarrassed or showing off. Several of the gallery's names do not match their drawings (standard P013 "legs dangling"
  is really a V-sit), so every pick below was checked against the drawing.

## Where to start

The ones with the most stories behind them, in order: **tatami side-sit (P041–P046)**, **kneeling upright (P031)**,
**palms together (upper P016)**, **maneki-neko (upper P008)**, **the yawn (upper P023)**, **knees hugged (P004)**,
**out of breath (P067)**, **squat watching something (P063)**, and the **lying poses (P049–P058)** for home and beach
nights. Each must meet the World's no-clipping rule: hands and hips on the real floor, tatami or sand height
(`foot-ground.js`, `seat-support.js`), and no hand going through the body.
