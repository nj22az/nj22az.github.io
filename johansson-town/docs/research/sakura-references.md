## Evidence and limits

Japanese Miniature Scenes' README was read on 23 October: it describes nine real-geometry Three.js/TypeScript/Vite dioramas, ambient animations, touch controls, reduced-motion support and static hosting. Rainy Konbini's specific listed contents are shelves, cold cabinets, checkout, vending, umbrellas, rain and wet reflections. Its scene is for observation and has no characters; actor/working-shop acceptance is our responsibility.

The other small konbini projects were identified through their repository/topic descriptions and available READMEs. Their demos, geometry measurements, performance and source licences have not been audited here. No source code, assets, audio or architecture from these newly added references has been imported by this stage. Any future code/asset reuse needs a file-level licence check and preserved attribution. Original Sakura assets and the existing character system remain authoritative.

## Application order

1. Production-frame layout, products, equipment and actual Thuận/queue routes.
2. Complete original prop inventory, office/rest/storage and exterior/roof registration.
3. Day/night window readability, ordinary visit rhythm and weather detail, assessed in the actual game at desktop and phone sizes.

Atmosphere follows a functioning, coherent shop; it must not obscure clipping, blocked routes or floating stock. Compare the baseline and candidate with the same Look, clock, weather and camera.

## Gondola construction references - 23 October 2026

[Lozier upright](https://www.lozier.com/products/lozier-gondola/uprite/) describes steel welded framing, slots and levelling; its [product index](https://www.lozier.com/products/product-index/) lists hardboard/pegboard/woodgrain and wire back options. Used to distinguish construction materials from decorative finish in Sakura: steel trays/posts, laminate-faced hardboard backs, wood-effect end panels and clear PET retainers. These are general construction references, not a claim of exact 1997 Japanese manufacturer/model. No vendor drawings, code or assets imported.
