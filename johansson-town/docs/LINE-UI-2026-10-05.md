# Johansson Town LINE UI — 5 October 2026

The nominated reference is [DESIGN.md — LINE, in kzhrknt/awesome-design-md-jp](https://kzhrknt.github.io/awesome-design-md-jp/design-md/line/DESIGN.md), also available in its [GitHub source](https://github.com/kzhrknt/awesome-design-md-jp/blob/main/design-md/line/DESIGN.md). The shared presentation uses its green `#06c755`, white cards, pale `#f7f8f9` surfaces, black text, system/Arial/Noto fonts, 8px buttons, 12px cards and 48px inputs. Game panels keep their working layout, readable scroll areas and authored artwork.

`line-ui.css` supplies the common theme for the landing/help screens, HUD and door controls, Town book, activities, bags, dialogue, magazines, records, camera settings, photo studio and character creator. Shared SVG controls preserve accessible labels, product counts, magazine canvases and place descriptions. Incoming dialogue uses pale left bubbles; player replies use green right bubbles. The standalone `creator/` page loads the same theme and icon decoration.

The normal browser audit opens the landing guide and game, then uses Town book, settings, clock, places and field book controls. The expanded audit supplies inventory, conversation, magazine-rack and records presentation fixtures through the real activity functions. Camera settings, photo capture and character creator are opened through normal game menus. The expanded captures are UI acceptance evidence, not an on-foot tour or proof of a purchase. Phone contexts use `isMobile` and `hasTouch`; coarse-pointer mode is asserted.

Reproduce with `node tools/ui-layout-audit.mjs <mode>`, `node tools/ui-expanded-audit.mjs <mode> --url http://127.0.0.1:8767/johansson-town/`, and the portable `node tools/ui-standalone-creator-audit.mjs`, where mode is `desktop`, `phone`, `landscape` or `compact`. Reports and PNGs are under `output/ui-layout-audit/`, `output/ui-expanded-audit/` and `output/ui-standalone-creator/` in the run workspace.
