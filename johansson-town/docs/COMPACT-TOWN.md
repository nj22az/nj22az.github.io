# Compact neighbourhood

The oversized eastern loop is replaced with a short residential circuit beside
the existing shops and tea house. School, shrine, river and western jetty geometry,
interaction anchors and resident destinations move together. Buildings, interiors,
character sizes, movement speed and central harbour coordinates retain their scale.

Route centreline lengths (metres):

| Route | Previous | Compact |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Residential | 263.3 | 66.8 |
| Shrine approach | 56.1 | 28.1 |
| Eastern alley | 156.3 | 125.6 |
| River walk | 111.9 | 73.8 |

The school pupils and western fishermen use the relocated destinations. The shrine
caretaker uses the shortened approach. The map and navigation envelope changes
from 220 × 212 metres to 130 × 178 metres. This is the map envelope, not a claim
about the total area of walkable paving. The shrine still has its six-metre raised
landing; the working central pier, ramen and izakaya entrances are unchanged.

Validation covers routes to the school, shrine and both fishing locations,
registered room entrances, the central street, Kenji's escort, saves and resident
schedules. Old remote route locations are outside the playable ground. Browser
presentation and device performance still require a graphics-capable browser.
