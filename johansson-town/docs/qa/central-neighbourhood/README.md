# Central neighbourhood and Fujita waterfront revision

The twelve existing Kitahama house plots are relocated into compact blocks adjoining Main Street and the former Rainflower/school area. Seven western homes and Kitahama Heights occupy the former empty central plots. The eastern houses move closer to the town hall, rather than extending towards the water. Home IDs, households and interiors are retained; gates, numbered flat doors, stairs, service drops and the town map read the revised canonical rectangles.

The town-hall block moves four metres towards its entrance. Its rear grounds shorten by 6.5 metres; the power house, day tank, canopy, bicycle shelter, flagpole and associated colliders/entrances move together. A shared town-level ground surface supports the residential blocks, with graded banks to the island. Lane intersections are partitioned into non-overlapping surfaces. The old Rainflower zig-zag and crossing stubs are gone.

The island loses a further 7.15% of its land area: 48,465.125 to 44,997.625 square metres. Relative to the island before PR #187, the total reduction is 16.53%. The northern shore, coastal road, Fumoto approach and walking circuit move together; Mount Aoba and existing settlements remain. Sugar cane is moved out of the residential centre.

Fujita's old broad L-pier and its unused seaward spur are removed. Narrow timber access follows the clear western approach from the quay to a small landing under the shed. Individual boards, bearers and piles are drawn; the houseboat connection also has individual wooden boards. Ground support follows the same footprints. Fujita's houseboat, furniture, nightly routine and harbour shipping remain.
