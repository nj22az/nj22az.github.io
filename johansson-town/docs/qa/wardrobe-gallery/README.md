# The costume gallery's wardrobe: review pictures (29 October 2026)

World's own renders of the thirteen sets made from the costume gallery (`docs/research/costume-gallery.md`), each on
Ms Ganaha in its first colourway. None of the gallery's pictures is here, or anywhere in the repository: its terms forbid
redistributing them.

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `sets-front.jpg` | All thirteen from the front (three-quarter): top row `tiered-sunday`, `cherry-bench`, `shirt-dress`, `linen-trousers`, `laundry-round`, `late-bowl`, `pier-watch`; bottom row `gingham-holidays`, `haarii-day`, `january-classroom`, `verandah-january`, `trading-round`, `celebration-dress`. |
| `sets-back.jpg` | The same from behind: the tiers, the gingham carried round the back and the sleeves, the caps on the hair. |
| `sets-walk.jpg` | Mid-stride from the side: the tiered and long skirts going with the legs. |
| `studio-cherry-bench.jpg` | Johansson Studio's Edit character (`/avatar-creator/`), Ms Ganaha wearing Cherry bench: the search, the "Worn by … · why" line and the three colourways. |

Made with `tools/wardrobe-sheet.html` (served from `johansson-town/`):
`tools/wardrobe-sheet.html?body=Ms%20Ganaha&turn=0.3&sets=<ids>`, with `&back=1` or `&pose=walk&turn=1.2`.

Looked at, frame by frame, before sending: no seams through the cloth, the gingham off the skin above the sundress, the
tiers stepping cleanly front and back. Known and not new (recorded in the handover): hands at rest lie a few centimetres
into every long skirt at the hips on feminine bodies, and a pale cap shows the hair cut flat under it at the back, which
is why the sets' caps are dark.
