SAKURA SHOTEN - REV E / 23 OCTOBER 2026

Sakura-Shoten-3D.html is a self-contained interactive preview. Open it in a modern
browser. No installation or network access is needed. Includes diorama, entrance,
clerk, office and roof cameras; wall, acoustic-ceiling, sign and grid controls.
Clerk eye height is schematic and adjustable; no substitute avatar is introduced.

Sakura-Konbini-CAD.pdf: three A3 review sheets.
Sakura-Konbini-CAD.dxf: editable R2010 CAD, one unit = one millimetre.
Sakura-Konbini-Plan.svg: vector ground-floor sheet.
Sakura-Konbini-Layout.json: revised coordinate schedule.
build_sakura_cad.py: reproducible drawing generator (ReportLab + ezdxf).
Sakura-CAD-Checks.json / Sakura-3D-Checks.json: completed validation.

prototype/interior.mjs exports buildSakuraShotenInterior(THREE).
prototype/index.html can be served with its local Three.js and font files.
The repository version uses the existing shared Three.js r170 rather than a CDN.
Japanese sign font is a Noto Sans JP subset, licensed under the SIL Open Font
License. Three.js is MIT-licensed. Notices are included and embedded in the
self-contained preview.

The complete rear strip, eight-door back-loaded cooler, long west chiller,
three islands, freezer and checkout spine follow the supplied layout with the
coordination corrections recorded in the handover and PDF. The rear door at
X=11,500 is a proposed relocation. Future ATM footprint is reserved.
Single storey only; roof stair and water tank remain service/escape access.

Review only. The live Johansson World game has not been changed or deployed.
