SHIOSAI HOUSEBOAT — SCREENSHOT AUDIT
18 October 2026

VERDICT
Gameplay integration passes the exercised route and schedule checks. Visual acceptance is withheld: the pillow does not support the sleeping resident, the stored futon is hidden inside a solid cupboard, and the third-person cabin camera gives a poor view of the interior.

The separate geometry images use the production boat, resident and sleep routine under diagnostic lighting; they do not represent the game's lighting or cel-shading pipeline.

FINDINGS

1. Pillow is behind the sleeping head — visual defect, medium priority.
Evidence: geometry/04-sofa-sleep-overhead.png; geometry/06-cabin-aisle.png; game/phone-sleep-overhead.png.
The pillow is clearly separated from the head. Its longitudinal centre is about 0.6 m beyond the resident's head centre. The resident's body is on the sofa, but the pillow is not functioning as head support.
Source: src/world/fujita-houseboat.js, Creased sofa pillow; HOUSEBOAT.bed; the actual Mr Fujita avatar measurements.
Required correction: align the pillow to the final sleeping head position, including its height and soft contact, then inspect overhead and side views again.

2. Folded futon is not visible — visual defect, medium priority.
Evidence: geometry/04-sofa-sleep-overhead.png and geometry/06-cabin-aisle.png.
The radio stands on a solid wooden cuboid. The folded futon and quilt meshes sit inside that opaque cuboid. Mesh-name and existence checks pass, but those items cannot be seen through the intended cupboard opening because no opening exists.
Source: src/world/fujita-houseboat.js, Futon cupboard, Folded futon, Stored futon quilt.
Required correction: use separate sides, back and shelves with an open front; verify that the folded bedding can actually be seen from the aisle without introducing new obstructions.

3. Third-person cabin view is cramped and obstructed — visual usability defect, medium priority.
Evidence: camera/phone-normal-cabin-camera.png and camera/desktop-normal-cabin-camera.png, compared with the alternate-camera images.
The roof is correctly hidden and both first- and third-person lens probes report blocked=false. Nevertheless, the close third-person camera fills a large part of the view with the player's head. A ceiling fitting also occupies the central sightline, conspicuously so on the phone-sized frame. This is a composition/visibility problem rather than evidence of a camera penetrating a solid wall. First-person view shows the cabin much more clearly.
Required correction: review houseboat-specific camera distance/framing and the treatment of roof-mounted fittings during cutaway; repeat real movement and both view modes after any change.

4. Night-time furnishings are hard to read — lower-priority polish concern.
Evidence: game/desktop-sleep-night.png and game/phone-sleep-night.png.
The sleeping cabin is very dark, particularly on the desktop capture. This is not a failed render or missing geometry. Review whether restrained material/ambient treatment could preserve the sleeping atmosphere while allowing the resident and furnishings to remain legible; avoid reintroducing expensive lights or render targets on iPhone.

5. Optional character polish.
Fujita sleeps with his work boots on. This is visible in both the diagnostic and game overhead views. It is not a collision failure; decide whether this is intentional characterisation before changing it.

PASSED / OBSERVED
- Loaded hull, extended stern, cabin and deck form a coherent exterior in the captured views.
- The desktop control test reached all eight waypoints across the gangway, through the cabin entrance and aisle, and back to the pier.
- Both viewport runs reached the sleeping state after 23:30 and the watching state after the 06:00 return.
- The resident's sleeping body is on the sofa; no obvious wall or galley penetration appears in the valid sleeping close-ups.
- Roof cutaway is active with the player inside, in both normal camera modes.
- Both tested first-person and third-person camera lens positions report blocked=false.
- Captured scene transforms were finite. Recorded browser/page/request errors were empty in the full-game and corrected-camera reports.
- Earlier numerical cargo-clearance and runtime/source tests remain separate evidence; screenshots alone do not prove clearance throughout every ship movement.

CAPTURE QUALITY CONTROL
Four initial captures are excluded from this delivery: desktop/phone sleep-staged-daylight and desktop/phone cabin-pedestrian. The attempted staging changed the routine state, and the nominal pedestrian capture did not advance simulation enough to restore the normal camera. An apparent head-through-roof image in that sequence is therefore not used as proof of a normal-play defect. The camera/ captures replace those invalid camera views. The diagnostic geometry images are explicitly labelled as isolated, fixed-lighting views.

Phone-sized Chromium screenshots do not establish physical Safari acceptance. No game source fix was applied as part of this screenshot audit; the findings above remain to be corrected.
