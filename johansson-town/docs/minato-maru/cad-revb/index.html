Units, scale and design status

Physical CAD geometry is at 1:1 in millimetres. PDF physical views are at 1:50 or 1:100 as stated on each sheet; system and electrical diagrams are not to scale (NTS). Print PDFs at 100% and check the scale bar before measuring.

CAD X is forward from the transom, Y is to port from the centreline, and Z is upward from the baseline. Manufacturer comparison data and proposed machinery, maintenance, removal and tank reservations do not establish an approved installation.

The archive is preserved as issued. Its game integration notes describe the issue-time status; subsequent runtime and publication status are recorded separately in the publication record.

Remaining work includes weight and stability reconciliation, structural design, supplier interfaces, physical piping, overhaul and lifting, protected escape, ventilation and electrical starting and protection studies. The construction work register identifies the required evidence.
