## Owner rejection and self-audit — 24 October 2026

Owner: “Audit yourself it’s far from the reference sophistication.” **Reference likeness is FAILED. The skirt is not visually accepted.** This supersedes any implication that the previous “Tailored skirt” delivery fulfilled the visual brief. Technical passing checks remain true, but are not aesthetic acceptance. Audited local implementation `eba91c4`, handover `670f6bb`; branch remains `codex/vy-reference-avatar`, local only.

### Findings grounded in the actual model, renders and source

1. **Primitive form, not developed garment form — high priority.** `skirt-shape.js` revolves one radius profile and the builder scales its depth uniformly. Front and back are the same underlying form. It cannot produce independently designed abdomen/seat contours or panel shaping. Reducing flare and adding rows did not address this limitation. A valid closed mesh is not evidence of a convincing skirt.
2. **The waistband claim was overstated — high priority.** The implementation rounds the top edge of the shell. It does not construct a distinct waistband or a convincing sewn/tucked transition to the shirt. Calling that “tailored” suggested more garment construction than the render contains. The supplied reference visibly separates the shirt, waist and skirt more clearly. Do not invent reference pockets, fastenings or stitching that have not been observed.
3. **Proportions are chosen formulas, not a measured reference fit — high priority.** The constants `.55 * hips`, `.78 * thigh` and `.008 * k` were authored by eye and then validated against their own expectations. The result reads as a broad, squat, nearly rectangular dark block relative to the reference. The avatar’s broader pelvis and different head/body proportions contribute. Uniform width/depth scaling is insufficient evidence of good fit across bodies.
4. **Surface response remains materially different — high priority.** The reference GLB contains base-colour, normal and metallic/roughness maps and visibly softer light gradients. The shared plain skirt is a single vertex colour through the existing cel garment material, sampling the blank garment UV strip. No skirt-specific fabric surface was authored. Its broad dark fill and thick outline lose volume and edge subtlety. The reference’s total polygon count is not the issue to solve; appropriate form, normals, material response and edge treatment are.
5. **Motion sophistication is unproven.** `towel-fit.js` uses averaged leg angles, analytic shear/lap shaping and collision pushes. That is a practical deformation approximation, not fabric simulation or an authored cloth-performance system. Single posed exports cannot establish appealing continuous motion. The supplied GLB has no skeleton or animation, so its motion cannot be claimed as a benchmark or as already reproduced.
6. **The comparison and acceptance method were inadequate — process failure.** The reference was rendered as its PBR material under one lighting setup; the avatar uses the Studio warm/cel pipeline and its own proportions/framing. That mixes form and rendering differences. The 20 passing tests cover topology, chosen dimensions, wardrobe budgets, contact checks and runtime integrity. None compares the result with measured reference geometry or independently establishes visual similarity. The specification tests mostly confirm the formulas I chose. I treated those checks as stronger evidence of success than they are.
7. **Whole-character differences remain visible.** Oversized head, narrow/simple limbs, thick outlines, block-like hair and the painted vest still differ substantially from the source. A skirt-only change cannot honestly be presented as achieving the reference character’s overall sophistication. Keep the requested skirt work scoped, but state this limitation rather than hiding it in a favourable comparison.

### Required replacement approach

- First measure the actual skirt in front, side and rear views: waistband height, skirt length, front/back/side profiles, waist/hip/hem width and depth, and edge thickness, normalised to the reference wearer. Record those measurements and their source. Use an isolated garment comparison, not only whole-character thumbnails. The source GLB is one combined mesh; careful garment selection is required before treating measurements as skirt-only.
- Author an original garment mesh with independently shaped front, back and side panels and a distinct waistband/turned hem. A low-poly production mesh with deliberate topology and controlled normals can carry this form; importing the whole 603k-triangle static character or merely increasing lathe subdivisions is not the solution. Preserve the shared avatar rig and editable recipe system.
- Fit that authored garment to the wearer with controlled waist, hip, seat, length and depth adjustments. Validate broad/slim and young/old bodies as real posed meshes, not just numerical specification objects. Do not prescribe one reference person’s body shape to the whole cast.
- Develop the cloth’s soft surface/edge response within Johansson’s cartoon direction. Compare neutral clay geometry separately from final materials, then compare both under matched cameras, scale and light. Do not add decorative seams or texture as a substitute for correcting the form, and do not change every accepted shader globally to conceal a garment defect.
- Validate continuous walking, turning and sitting footage, with frame-by-frame hip, thigh, hand, hem and seat contact checks. Then rerun technical checks and obtain visual acceptance. Preserve the earlier paired-pose blocker until separately solved.

Proposed visual gate for the next prototype: reference-wearer skirt waist/hip/hem dimensions and length within 5% of measured targets, with the measurement convention recorded; readable separate waistband and thin rounded hem at 512 px; clear front/side/back garment volume; matched-light front/quarter/side/back comparisons; continuous motion review across representative bodies. These are next-iteration targets, not results achieved here. Owner approval remains the final appearance judgement.

Audit-only follow-up: inspected the actual supplied GLB metadata, saved reference render, current front/quarter skirt renders, geometry construction, shared cloth shader and test assertions. No new geometry, material or gameplay change; no new test run claimed. Next action is a measured garment reconstruction, not another radius adjustment. No publication attempted.
