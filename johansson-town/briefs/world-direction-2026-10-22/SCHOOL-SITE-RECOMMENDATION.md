## Preferred option among the measured cleared plots

**Kitahama northern garden/parking block**, world x 16–42, z 62–72: 26 × 10 m, 260 m². It is the only continuous measured northern cleared block in this comparison large enough to investigate a consolidated single-storey programme without joining across existing lanes. It has lane frontage on both long sides and an eastern approach connection. That makes separate public arrival and kitchen servicing plausible, not verified safe.

Keep the existing 9 × 7.2 m classroom interior. Add teachers' preparation/office, community kitchen and meal space, community administration, toilets/wash, stores and sheltered circulation. A 22 × 8 m study envelope would occupy 176 m² gross, leaving only 84 m² of the parcel before external access, edging, refuse, servicing and landscaping. This is arithmetic for a test fit, not a room layout or proof of capacity. A meaningful protected playground/outdoor learning area remains unresolved; do not present the parcel as sufficient for the whole school without it.

The block is occupied by a shared vegetable garden and four monthly parking bays (three assigned: Kōji, Dr Ken and Mac). Preserve those useful functions with demonstrated replacement capacity and routes before removal. A kitchen teaching garden may be a useful successor, but is not an automatic replacement for the residents' garden. Do not assume the freed old school/hall footprints can accept cars: check clinic access, power-house compound, fuel tank, visitors and servicing first.

## Arrival and game integration to prove

- Keep the northern spine/approach loop, neighbourhood footpath, household gates and rubbish/cane-lorry routes connected. The source lanes are narrow shared streets; a lane label does not prove a safe school route.
- Prefer a recessed pedestrian gate and enclosed forecourt; assess kitchen delivery/refuse from a separate edge without forcing children into vehicles' path. Determine crossings from actual camera and walking views.
- Preserve the clinic, doctor's accommodation and power-house compound. Avoid an educational entrance beside tank/service access.
- Preserve the classroom desks, teacher/student routines and room behaviour; separate community evening access from teaching space. Reconcile Emi's duties.
