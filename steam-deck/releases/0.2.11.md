# Johansson World Linux 0.2.11

Brings the browser world of 20 October 2026 (main 59736931, 107 commits after the 4bc264e5 that 0.2.10 shipped) to the offline edition.

- Sakura's upstairs is a small shop's office now, not a big empty floor: Thuan's desk under the harbour window, the kitchenette, the filing cabinet and the safe, and exactly two outlooks, Main Street from the front and the ferry berth from the side. Behind a sliding door is a small tatami rest room with a futon in the cupboard. The street front shows what is behind it: the office's windows, the rest room's shoji (lit at dusk) and plain wall elsewhere.
- Sakura's small office beside the till is rebuilt: Thuan's desk with the open sales ledger, a desk lamp, a mini fridge with the TV on top (ferry arrivals, the weather, Riku's game), the cat clock, the cork board, the October 1997 calendar and the rota, fairy lights and her chair. On Linux the lamp, fridge, TV and chair work, and the ledger opens the live books.
- You can tap your way into Sakura's office and rest room. A tap on the stairs, at the desk or on the futon now finds a path through the office door.
- Sakura's stairs: the camera follows you up and down both flights with all of you and the steps ahead in frame, frames you and the next flight on the turning landing, and no longer ends up in the toilet. A ceiling light that hung in mid-air over the stairwell is moved into the stockroom, and the stair guards no longer stop the camera as if they were 5 m tall.
- Sakura loads one storey at a time. Upstairs, the shop floor below is put away and its customers, coolers and stock are not run (unless someone is in the stockroom you can see down into); back downstairs it all returns.
- The escape-ladder sign on Sakura's back wall reads in full (ESCAPE LADDER / KEEP CLEAR), and the meter cupboard and extractor sit on the wall instead of 2 cm off it.
- No more flashing surfaces across Minato. Faces that lay in the same plane and flickered against each other are fixed at Sakura (the automatic door, the signboard, the office storey), the izakaya, Sato Ramen, Nakamura Zenzai, the Okinawan quarters, Main Street's kerbs, the quay, the bookshop, the police station, the school, the port building and the moored boats.
- Minato Police Station gets a proper 1990s kei patrol car in black and white, and a bicycle rack in its yard.
- Every parked bicycle is one proper model (frame, spoked wheels, mudguards, stand, basket where it belongs), each with an owner and a reason. Bicycles no longer stand inside planters, walls or washing.
- The dock workshop: Tetsuo's electrical bench (scope, bench supply, soldering iron, lamp, fan and isolating switch, all working on Linux) and Chin's wall of spanners, outboard spares and a test tank. The workshop's and the bookshop's name boards hang as proper sign plates.
- Around town: a coral-stone bank with chain bollards along the west shore north of the Nishi seawall; Kitahama's drinks machines against the Heights' wall; period adverts on Sakura's side walls; the J-Coffee banner on the quay and a Sakura poster inside Minato; the school yard's lane laid on the yard; sand graded where buildings meet the beach step; a kerb cut so Thuan can walk off the boat at the outer pier.
- Residents: Mrs Nakamura and Mai climb the stair to their home above the dessert shop at night. Sakura's customers queue at the till, residents stand at their own spots on the street and the quay (Thuan sweeps her doorstep, Jack does his quay round) and take the quay's free spots in turn on their breaks. After a load Sakura's clerk is found at the till, not walking in from the door. A new pair pose, back to back.
- The browser edition is now a lighter demo of this game: it simulates only the residents you can see, gives short greetings and shows trade and machinery as display pieces. The Linux edition keeps running everything: every resident's whole day, their chats and happenings, full conversations, Sakura's trade and stock, the machinery and the live ledgers.
- The Linux edition keeps its native-resolution path, its detail-stream limits, its edition line on the title, controller mapping, Back/Menu cancellation, HUD isolation, saved settings, profile data and the bed. The installer is pinned to 0.2.11. Compatible 0.2.10 installations can receive a verified file patch when the Electron runtime matches; published versions are retained.

Checked here: the desktop controller/save/updater/installer/life tests, the production runtime build and its package test, the web suites named in the release journal, the controller repair acceptance in a real browser, and the packaged offline Linux application started under a virtual display with its built-in smoke check. Not verified here: physical Steam Deck controller, suspend/resume, GPU performance and physical-device acceptance.
