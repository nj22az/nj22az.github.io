# Johansson World Linux 0.2.18

Brings the offline Linux edition up to the current browser World of 24 October 2026, and adds the controller-settings repair that 0.2.17 left out.

- **Looks.** The Look setting gains Miniature, Woodblock print, Watercolour, Oil painting and Cartoon, and the print and paint looks Risograph, Pencil Manga, Heavy Brush Oil and Rubber Stamp. The shared illustration shaders are improved (cleaner ink, Manga, Cartoon and Oil), and the drawn looks give eyes and brows solid black marks. Every start is in the Natural look.
- **Town book.** A home screen of icon tiles, its button within thumb reach; fifteen tiles folded into eight and shorter place cards. The Look picker and Free camera bar use pictures and icons instead of text.
- **The town.** Nhung is the island doctor; Higa Liquor has closed and Sakura sells and supplies all the drink. Vy has a new look, from the owner's reference model. Johansson's eyes and speech stay animated in greetings and conversations.
- **Sakura.** The Eisa festival poster says Eisa, and the cold cabinet stands the way its notes say: tea, water and cola at eye level, children's drinks low, milk on top, beer low by the till.
- **Controllers.** A saved Look or Move stick pair that points past the connected pad's axes (as an xpad layout saved as axes 3 and 4 can) now falls back to the default sticks, so the right stick still turns the camera and the left stick still walks.

Physical Steam Deck, Bluetooth-controller and iPhone acceptance remain unverified. Version 0.2.17 and its assets are preserved.

Use the in-game Settings → Updates check or the published installer. Existing save profiles are retained. Release and software-acceptance evidence is recorded in `HANDOVER.md` (24 October 2026 session note).
