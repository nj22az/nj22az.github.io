# Johansson World Linux 0.2.16

Bluetooth controllers on Linux, on the published 0.2.15 (PR #340).

- A pad Chromium does not map (often a Bluetooth pad, whose product id differs from the same pad on a cable) is now read in its Linux driver's layout: xpad/xpadneo, hid-playstation, hid-nintendo and hid-generic. Triggers and the D-pad hat become buttons, and the face buttons keep their places. Before, a resting trigger turned the camera on its own, Start did not open the menu and the D-pad did nothing.
- The pad being pressed takes over from an idle one, such as Steam's virtual pad, without pausing as "Controller disconnected". A pad chosen in Settings still wins.
- The controls page shows "Linux driver layout" for such a pad.
- The world, the ferry timetable, saves and the 0.2.14 caption fix are unchanged from 0.2.15.

Not yet verified: this has not been tried on a real Bluetooth pad. Which controller and distribution the owner uses is still to be confirmed. Physical Steam Deck acceptance remains unmeasured.
