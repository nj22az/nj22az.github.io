# Johansson World Linux 0.2.12

Brings the browser world of 21 October 2026 (main 3f5b4f49, 40 commits after the 59736931 that 0.2.11 shipped) to the offline edition.

- One ground system across the island. Every road, pavement, quay, lane, path, lawn and beach now follows one rulebook of surfaces, heights, colours and edges, and is laid by one ground builder. Main Street has dark asphalt, reddish pavers and 15 cm kerbs, dropped with ramps and warning blocks at the crossings and the bus stop. The quays stand at their own level with their copings. Kitahama's and Nishi-machi's back lanes are asphalt over concrete with U-gutters along the yards, and the west yard and the town hall's grounds are laid the same way. The beach is coral-white sand with a wet band at the water. Harbour Park, the east lawn, Kitano Road's footway and Kitahama's coastal walk have proper edged walks, and no building stands on a stray ring of paving.
- A quiet HUD that leaves the picture to the town: one small chip for place and time, no Run button over the picture (push the stick almost all the way to run; the run button still works), a small action pill that fades in and out, the HUD dimming after a few seconds without input, and Clean view (H, or the town book) to hide every control.
- Sakura's stair is now a passage into the dark: step onto it and you walk into the dark, and come out in the office upstairs (or back down in the stockroom). The office is entered only from inside the shop. Its fire door opens only from inside, and "Evacuate by the fire escape" takes you down the caged ladder to the yard. The office has its own warm tube light, and the flashing faces in the hall and the rest room are fixed.
- The parked cars at Kitahama are rebuilt like the traffic: real wheel openings, a tapered cabin, bumpers, lamps, mirrors and yellow kei plates, each reversed into its own bay. Dr Ken's tall kei car, Mac's car with its bonnet open over the engine, and Kōji's kei truck with his ice chest.
- Nakamura Zenzai is a proper 1990s shop-house, with Japanese signs (中村ぜんざい), wooden menu tags, and a noren, parasol and ice flag that are out only while the counter is open. Red lanterns glow from dusk. After hours a roller shutter rolls down over the counter, and rolls up again in the morning.
- Higa Liquor (比嘉酒店) and the coin laundry (コインランドリー) come to life. Grandmother Higa keeps the counter (Yui on Saturdays) to the shop's hours, open in the morning and the evening, with a card on the door between and the shutter at night. Tetsuo and Mac sit on the evening bench on their own days. Ms Ganaha keeps the laundry and folds the washing. Residents do their washing on their own days, the machines run on the clock, and you can do your own wash. The laundry's noticeboard carries real story hooks, the week's calendar and the weather.
- Minato Police Station is upgraded: Mori's balcony with bedding and towels out on dry days, a breeze-block screen, services and an LP gas hutch, a new canopy sign, nameplate and door handles, and a noticeboard with real lost-property stories and his patrol hours.
- Harbour Park is reworked: whole lawn beds, a 港公園 map board with an English guide, a real koi pond whose koi gather when fed, the footbath on the bath's hours, flower beds kept by Thuan, Mrs Nakamura and Grandmother Higa, and a gajumaru tree with a bench on the beach lawn.
- The fish market's drinks machine stands against the ice plant's wall instead of loose among the crates, and the ice plant's sign reads 製氷.
- Doors lead only into rooms that work. The Nakamura family's stair no longer traps you on entry, and you no longer arrive stuck against the gangway rail at the dock.
- The Linux edition keeps running everything: every resident's whole day, their chats and happenings, full conversations, the trade and stock, the machinery and the live ledgers. It also keeps its native-resolution path, its detail-stream limits, its edition line on the title, controller mapping, Back/Menu cancellation, HUD isolation, saved settings, profile data and the bed. The installer is pinned to 0.2.12. Compatible 0.2.11 installations can receive a verified file patch when the Electron runtime matches; published versions are retained.

Checked here: the desktop controller/save/updater/installer/life tests, the production runtime build and its package test, the web suites named in the release workflow, the edition, ground-standard, enterable-sites, Higa Liquor and laundry, drinks-machine placement and police station suites, and the packaged offline Linux application started under a virtual display with its built-in smoke check. Not verified here: the controller repair acceptance in a real browser, physical Steam Deck controller, suspend/resume, GPU performance and physical-device acceptance.
