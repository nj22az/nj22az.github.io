# Johansson World Linux 0.2.10

Integrates the browser world as of 19 October 2026 (main f22b1a97, 118 commits after the a0e0a25 that 0.2.9 shipped), on top of 0.2.9's graphics, memory and loading fixes.

- The island's people, consolidated: 26 residents in 14 households. Ten supporting residents have retired from the island (their drawings are kept for history); the ferry's chief officer, deck hand, purser and cook and the Kitano line hand are people now, with names and homes (Ryo, Ren, Aya, Jun, Kai); Mr Nakandakari is Mac, the relief engineer. ☰ → People lists exactly these 26, each with an owned bed.
- Nine emptied buildings are gone and their streets compacted, and every cleared lot is dressed with a purpose (Higa Liquor's crate yard on Main Street among them). Minato Police Station stands on the lot beside the old kōban: Officer Mori works there and lives there.
- The docks: the main quay dressed for work, two fishing boats on the east quay, the island cargo boat to the ferry's finish, Fujita's pier rebuilt as a timber pier, a cargo staging bay and a fuel point on the kerb.
- Sakura Shōten is Thuan's outright, with shop hours; flowers are sold at Sakura and post at the port window. Chin is the ferry's motorman and the workshop's repairman, with no shift at Sakura; Fujita masters the ship, sober on watch.
- Tempers: six comedy-anger poses and three faces. Moves (walk, run, dash, skip and tiptoe) and pair poses (hug, kiss, fight, shove) with their faces, on every body.
- Stronger lamp pools on the streets, the outer pier and the warehouse; cleaner street circuits; the arrival camera never comes down through a roof. The resident guide, portraits and photo shoots follow the 26. The New Yokosuka research notes ship in docs/.
- Old saves load. Records under old names move to the live person, retired residents' records are dropped, and a save made inside a removed home opens at the retained public place (the web's CAST_REVISION 2 migration). The Linux character selection follows it: a 0.2.9 save playing as a renamed resident goes on as that person at their own checkpoint, a retired one falls back to Johansson, and a saved outdoor position that a new building now covers is moved to clear ground.
- The Linux edition keeps its native-resolution path (device pixel ratio 1, no supersampling), its wider detail-stream limits, its edition line on the title, controller mapping, Back/Menu cancellation, HUD isolation, saved settings and profile data. The installer is pinned to 0.2.10 (0.2.9's installer still fetched 0.2.8). Compatible 0.2.9 installations can receive a verified file patch when the Electron runtime matches; published versions are retained.

Checked here: the desktop controller/save/updater/installer/life tests, the production runtime build and its package test, the web suites for the docks, the police station, the cast and its migration, the households, the pair poses, the lot dressing, the English inventory, loading, recovery, detail streaming, context actions, the controller game and the power wires, and the assembly of the offline site (the desktop patches applied, Google Fonts stripped, every asset the web added present). These are automated checks on the merged source. Not verified here: the packaged Electron application (no Electron binary in this environment, so no native smoke, disk-save or four-size console acceptance), physical Steam Deck controller, suspend/resume, GPU performance and physical-device acceptance.
