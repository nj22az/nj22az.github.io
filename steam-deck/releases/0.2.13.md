# Johansson World Linux 0.2.13

Updates the offline edition from main b51ef619 (21 October 2026).

- Separate Minato school, Community Hall and clinic buildings, with passages between them, revised Japanese signs and a fenced power-house compound.
- The former mayor office is retired. Nils has a study at home on Kitano-jima; the Community Hall keeps the self-service town register and accounts. Existing saves migrate to the retained Community Hall.
- Refined town-hall grounds, beach shoreline, wet-sand blending and coastal foam.
- Compact shared Minato Izakaya / Sato Ramen interior, revised service and seating layout, and a walnut counter and back bar with fictional liquor brands.
- Seated characters can take a sober drink with the new shared drinking animation; the diagnostic side-view simulator is included. This does not add new drunken behaviour.
- The cloud audit startup fix now draws the title before START, including the Linux runtime.
- Linux keeps the full resident simulation, trade, machinery and live ledgers, alongside its controller menus, owned beds, playable residents, saved settings and existing save profile. The installer targets 0.2.13. Compatible 0.2.12 installations may receive a verified patch when the runtime matches.

Corrections and limits: the ferry's independent timetable is a brief, not implemented in this release. The 0.2.12 statement that Tetsuo and Mac reliably occupy Higa Liquor's evening bench was too strong: Mac's ferry duty and Tetsuo's car errand can prevent it. Park tending times remain data, without fully connected tending routines. Physical Steam Deck performance, suspend/resume and controller acceptance have not been measured here.

The release workflow checks controller/save/updater/installer/life tests, builds the runtime and complete offline package, exercises controller repair, starts the native Linux application under a virtual display, and verifies a fresh installation against its published checksum before the update is considered complete.
