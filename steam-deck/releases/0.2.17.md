# Johansson World Linux 0.2.17

The ordinary island game is peaceful. This release withdraws the current fighting system from the offline Linux edition, matching the approved browser change.

- Festival bouts, tournaments, spectating, insult-triggered quarrels, attack controls, first-person weapons and combat rewards are removed. Normal conversations, apologies, peaceful festival activities, dining, exploration and ferry operations remain.
- The combat-dependent cave descent is closed; its mouth and reading interactions remain.
- Old combat progress is retired without removing money, possessions, relationships, character identity or travel milestones. Active/imported fight, arena or cave saves recover at the safe harbour opening with ordinary controls and HUD.
- Includes the accepted current main World, including independent izakaya food service and seating improvements, and the added frozen-render and save-consolidation regression coverage.

No replacement fighting mode is included. The separate proposed controller-settings repair is not part of this release. Physical Steam Deck, Bluetooth-controller and iPhone acceptance remain unverified. Version 0.2.16 and its assets are preserved.

Use the in-game Settings → Updates check or the published installer. Existing save profiles are retained. Release and software-acceptance evidence is recorded in `johansson-town/briefs/HANDOFF-COMBAT-WITHDRAWAL-2026-10-22.md`.
