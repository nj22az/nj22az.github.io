    First conversion

    Three steps, entirely on your device.

    1. Choose a preset. Start with the intended use, then refine.
    2. Compare the result. Drag the divider and zoom into edges.

    Guide

    Choosing an engine

    VTracer
    Best general choice. Produces compact colour or monochrome paths and handles illustrations and photographs.
    Potrace
    Excellent for black-and-white logos, signatures and line art. It is included in the local vector worker under GPL-2.0.
    ImageTracer
    A pure JavaScript fallback. Useful where WebAssembly is restricted.

    Ctrl/⌘ + V pastes an image. Ctrl/⌘ + S downloads the current SVG.
