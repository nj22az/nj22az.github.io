Scandic Laajavuori, Jyväskylä: easy free parking and a spa with a real pool

Nils Johansson · 27 October 2026

One night at Scandic Laajavuori on Laajavuorentie in Jyväskylä, Finland, in a Standard room for 116 EUR.

Parking

This is how parking should be. The hotel’s outdoor car park is large and free: 300 spaces, with engine heaters at some of them. There is nothing to book and nothing to pay. Park the car and walk in.

Charging an electric car costs extra, through the Plugit app.

The spa

A free spa is included with the stay, and it has a proper swimming pool. The hotel describes 800 m² of spa with jacuzzis, swimming pools, a pool for small children and several saunas, and the bathrobes are in the room.

A bonus at check-in

They did not ask to reserve anything on my card. Some hotels always ask for your card, for a deposit or in case you order something to the room. Here, nothing.

Verdict

Free parking: green. Easy free parking, and a spa with a real pool, all in the 116 EUR for the night.
