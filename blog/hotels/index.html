Hotel Marina — Vedbæk

Vedbæk Strandvej 391, 2950 Vedbæk, Denmark
Recorded 24 October 2026

Hotel Marina offers free outdoor guest parking at Vedbæk Strandvej 391. Register the vehicle on the APCOA tablet at reception; the hotel’s arrival information also confirms that reception-tablet registration is required.

The recorded accommodation payment was 5,728.46 kr for five nights, including taxes and fees after a booking credit. This works out to an average of 1,145.69 kr per night. The supplied receipt labels the currency only as “kr”, so no SEK or DKK currency code has been assumed.

Free parking makes Marina a useful addition to the parking shortlist. No separate parking payment is recorded. A broader assessment of the room, service and other facilities has not been supplied.

Scandic Elmia — Jönköping

Elmiavägen 8, 554 54 Jönköping, Sweden
Recorded 24 October 2026

Scandic Elmia provided a satisfactory overnight stay at a fair room price of SEK 1,107. Parking cost a further SEK 105, bringing the recorded total for the room and parking to SEK 1,212.

Parking was paid separately through Pair Parking’s website, where the parking receipt was also available. On this stay, the parking charge could not be included on the hotel receipt.

The room price was reasonable. The additional parking cost and separate receipt should be taken into account when comparing alternatives, particularly for business travel. This stay does not qualify for the free-parking shortlist.

What this register records

Parking cost, ease of parking and receipt arrangements are recorded separately. “Not assessed” means there is not yet enough first-hand information to judge that point. A hotel only appears under “Free and easy parking” when both conditions have been reported.

Stay reports and official hotel information are identified in each note. Personal names, booking references, card details and exact stay dates are excluded. Prices and parking arrangements can change; the recorded date keeps each entry in context.
