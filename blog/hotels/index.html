What this register records

Hotels join the free-parking list only after their own published information confirms free guest parking. Each parking entry links to its source and check date, with known registration requirements and paid alternatives explained. Coverage grows as hotels are verified.

Green means confirmed free guest parking, the preferred option and default filter. Yellow means “Easy parking”: parking costs money, but you can pay at the hotel. Red means paid parking handled separately through an external payment route — avoid. Payment at the hotel and inclusion on the hotel receipt are recorded separately.

Stay reports and official hotel information are identified in each note. Personal names, booking references, card details and exact stay dates are excluded. Prices and parking arrangements can change; the recorded date keeps each entry in context.
