Scandic Elmia — Jönköping

Elmiavägen 8, Jönköping, Sweden
By Nils Johansson · Recorded 24 October 2026

Scandic Elmia provided a satisfactory overnight stay at a fair room price of SEK 1,107. Parking cost a further SEK 105, bringing the recorded total for the room and parking to SEK 1,212.

Parking was paid separately through Pair Parking’s website, where the parking receipt was also available. On this stay, the parking charge could not be included on the hotel receipt.

The room price was reasonable. The additional parking cost and separate receipt should be taken into account when comparing alternatives, particularly for business travel. This stay does not qualify for the free-parking shortlist.

What this register records

Parking cost, ease of parking and receipt arrangements are recorded separately. “Not assessed” means there is not yet enough first-hand information to judge that point. A hotel only appears under “Free and easy parking” when both conditions have been reported.

The aim is to build a useful shortlist and, as the register grows, a map of hotels with free, straightforward parking. Prices and parking arrangements can change; the recorded date keeps each review in context.
