Scandic Elmia, Jönköping: a fair price, with parking and a card hold on the side

Nils Johansson · 25 October 2026

One night at Scandic Elmia on Elmiavägen in Jönköping. A satisfactory stay at a fair room price: SEK 1,107.

Parking

Parking cost another SEK 105, paid through Pair Parking’s website, with its own receipt there. It could not go on the hotel bill, so the stay came to SEK 1,212 on two receipts.

The card at check-in

At check-in they asked to reserve my credit card, even though I had already paid for the room and said I did not want a voucher.

Verdict

Paid parking: avoid. The room is fairly priced, but the separate parking receipt and the card hold add work to a business trip.
