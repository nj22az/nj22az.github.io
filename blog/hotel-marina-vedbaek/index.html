Hotel Marina, Vedbæk: free parking, registered at reception

Nils Johansson · 13 October 2026

I spent five nights at Hotel Marina on Vedbæk Strandvej in Denmark. On a field service trip I want parking that is simple and as little paperwork as possible. Marina has both.

Parking

The outdoor car park is free for guests. Register the car on the APCOA tablet at reception and that is it. The hotel’s own arrival page says the same.

There is no parking fee, so there is no parking receipt to keep.

What it cost

Five nights came to 5,728.46 kr, including taxes and fees, after a booking credit. That is about 1,146 kr a night.

Verdict

Free parking: green. Hotel Marina is on the free-parking list. This is about parking and price only, not a review of the room or the service.
