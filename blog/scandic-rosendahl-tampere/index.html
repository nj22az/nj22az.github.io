Scandic Rosendahl, Tampere: paid parking on one bill

Nils Johansson · 23 October 2026

Scandic Rosendahl is on Pyynikintie in Tampere, Finland. Its parking is not free, but it is still on my list, because it is easy.

“It’s easy to park there, and I know you can pay for parking at the hotel.”

Parking

You pay for parking at the hotel, and it goes on the hotel bill. One receipt covers the stay, with no separate parking receipt to keep.

The hotel’s published prices: outdoor parking EUR 2 an hour or EUR 8 for 24 hours, and the garage EUR 2.50 an hour or EUR 14 for 24 hours. You can also pay at a machine or with ParkMan or EasyPark, but the apps add their own fees. Prices change, so follow the signs on site.

Verdict

Easy: yellow. Paid, but paid at the hotel and on the hotel bill. It is the only Easy hotel in the register so far.
