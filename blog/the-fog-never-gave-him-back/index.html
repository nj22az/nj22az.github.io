The Fog Never Gave Him Back: the second film from Johansson Animation Studios

Nils Johansson · 22 November 2026

The second film from my animation studio is out. The Fog Never Gave Him Back picks up where Jack the Ripper left off: the man in the hat was never caught, and now a new singer, on a music-hall stage in the fog, tells how it ended.

She tells of a girl from Limehouse who was waiting in the alley, who stepped aside like a falling leaf and struck like thunder, and of a gentleman who went over into the dirty old river and never came back. It is a cartoon, so there is no blood: only fog, a fight with a lot of style, and a happy ending.

The song is my own. The characters were made with Meshy, the dolls were posed, lit and filmed in fog in Blender, and the film was cut in Remotion, with AI-assisted tools helping at every step. This one went further than the first: a full acting score for the singer, a fight choreographed to the beat, a cancan, and a lot of work to keep every hand, cape and boot where it belongs.

It is the second of a planned series of five.
