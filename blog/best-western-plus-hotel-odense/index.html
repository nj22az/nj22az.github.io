Best Western Plus Hotel Odense: free parking, but a long walk

Nils Johansson · 18 October 2026

Best Western Plus Hotel Odense, on Ørbækvej, has free parking. On paper that puts it on the green list. In practice it did not work.

Parking

The free parking was a long walk from the hotel, and it was on a construction site. That is awkward access for field service work.

The hotel’s own parking page explains why. The hotel is being extended, part of the car park is closed during the works, and guests are sent to the green areas on its parking map and to the signs on site.

Verdict

Avoid, for now. The parking is free, so this is about access, not cost. It is worth asking the hotel again once the works are finished.
