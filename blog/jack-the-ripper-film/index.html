Jack the Ripper: the first film from Johansson Animation Studios

Nils Johansson · 22 November 2026

This is the first film from my animation studio: Jack the Ripper (a killer is loose), an animated musical made to my own song.

A cabaret singer walks the gaslit streets of Whitechapel in 1888 and sings of the man in the hat, whose face nobody ever sees. The look I wanted was dolls on a stage in real fog: theatre light, a soft haze over everything, and the city as a backdrop to the singer.

The characters were made with Meshy, the scenes were built, lit and filmed in Blender, and the film was edited in Remotion, with AI-assisted tools throughout. The legend ends as history does: he was never caught.

The story continues in the second film, The Fog Never Gave Him Back.
