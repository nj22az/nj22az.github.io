## Reproduction and limits

Serve the repository root with `python3 -m http.server 8040`, then run:

```sh
node sjoskolan/verktyg/qa/week40-student-path.mjs
```

Install Playwright locally or set `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` to its installed module.
`BASE_URL`, `CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE` and `EVIDENCE_DIR` override the defaults.
In the constrained execution environment, Chromium headless shell was launched
with `CHROMIUM_SINGLE_PROCESS=1` to avoid unavailable Unix sockets; these flags
are optional elsewhere. Each viewport gets a fresh browser and isolated storage.

This validates the committed source in Chromium, not physical iPhone/iPad,
WebKit, the live Pages deployment or a physical camera scan. Rendered screenshot
QR decoding passed. The encrypted teacher import
is separately skipped without its password. The submission channel is selected
by the teacher; no test result was sent to anyone.
