For the lesson

Chapter 1 accompanies Monday 28 September: sinusoidal AC voltage. Chapter 2 accompanies Tuesday 29 September: reactance and impedance. Chapter 3 accompanies Friday 2 October: AC power.

Use the chapter buttons to start the relevant lesson. Pause at each calculation question and continue when students have written their method. Answers appear after a short thinking pause. The current Swedish presentations and guided exercises are linked from the week page.

This longer film was made from the earlier presentations. The revised week page defines the current basic tasks and optional extension work. Switch off the music when discussing a diagram, or use the transcript below.

Wave motion is slowed for explanation. Component and power-factor correction examples are simplified teaching models; practical work follows the course’s supervised laboratory arrangements.
