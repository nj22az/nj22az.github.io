For the lesson

Show a chapter, pause at the question and ask students to predict the result. Continue in the simulator or with the corresponding presentation slides. The complete transcript is available below. Switch off the music if you prefer to discuss the diagrams alongside the narration.

The diagrams are simplified teaching models. Practical connections are made during supervised laboratory work. Follow the actual instrument manual and the relevant safety procedures.

This English edition covers the same lesson content as the original Swedish film. The linked PowerPoint and simulator remain in Swedish.

Sources and production

Source: Multimeter och mätfel, Sjöskolan, 70 slides. The extension examples on slides 66–68 are included. Links to additional Swedish videos remain in the PowerPoint.

  • Hioki: measurement categories and electrical limits
  • Fluke: resolution and accuracy
  • Fluke: instrument loading

Measurement categories are described in the IEC 61010 series. A category rating is not a guarantee against misuse. The impulse tests shown are selected teaching examples, not permitted measuring voltages.

The characters, mascots and animation are drawn in JavaScript using HTML Canvas. Helios drives the frames through an adapted Anidoodle film core. The original music, Mätresan, is composed and synthesised in Java. British English narration is generated locally with Kokoro-82M, voice bf_emma. The voice is synthetic.
