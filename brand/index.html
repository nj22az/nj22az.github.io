On a web page

Add the script once, then place the logo anywhere. It takes its colours from the page.

<script src="https://nj22az.github.io/assets/brand/johansson-logo.js" defer></script>

<johansson-logo layout="signature" replay style="width:280px"></johansson-logo>

<!-- layout: signature | mark · dark: white version · static: no animation -->
<style> johansson-logo { --jl-ink: currentColor; } </style>

Or use a file directly: <img src="/assets/brand/johansson-signature-animated.svg" alt="Johansson">.

Colours

On light backgrounds: navy #1d2f57 with a gold star #d99a2b. On dark: white with a warm white star and gold glow #ffd98a. The intro's night blue runs from #1f3f7a to #0d1b3a. Keep clear space of half the logo's height on every side.
