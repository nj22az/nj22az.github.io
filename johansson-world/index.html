A place to spend time

Find your own way around.

There is more here than a start screen. Johansson World brings together a walkable town, everyday routines, conversations and the things people make.

01 · THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

Take the long way home.

Explore the streets, harbour and residential lanes. Visit Sakura Shōten, browse the shops and pause at Umi-no-yu. Doors lead to interiors with their own details and people.

02 · THE EVERYDAY

See what the day brings.

Talk to residents, follow their routines, fish, collect small finds and discover the stories behind ordinary objects. Shops and people follow the town’s clock.

03 · THE WATERFRONT

Meet Minato Maru.

The ferry connects the harbour with life across the water. Its terminal, working deck and journeys are part of the world, with more island hopping planned.

04 · YOUR OWN PACE

Keep a little of the island.

Use the Town book to find your bearings and manage your progress. Create a character, take photographs and return to the places and people you remember.

What comes next

A world that keeps growing.

The Linux edition is the current focus. Its development journal tracks differences from the web edition and which improvements may eventually be shared.

A console feel

Clearer menus, stick movement and camera controls, a first-play guide and configurable controls are part of the Linux candidate.

Lives of their own

Playable residents, assigned homes and beds, and rare quarrels following repeated insults are Linux candidate features.

A better island

A coherent road grid, a more compact Minato and a small northern fishing village are proposals. Future ferry destinations should each have a reason to visit.
