# The owner's photographs: what each shows, and what it gives the Chisum

Johansson Shipyard · reference · 11 November 2026

The owner sent these during the local session of 11 November 2026 ("here are details you can add to the wheelhouse", "here
are more ship details", "more various details that could fit into the Chisum"). **The pictures themselves are not in the
repository**: they came through the conversation and a session does not keep them. This is the written record, made while
they could be seen. If the owner puts the files in a folder, link them here by number.

People appear in some of them; they are not described or named here. Ship and maker names are given only where they can be
read in the picture.

How to use it: a row is a detail to be drawn as it is (JSY-STD-404: its parts, its fastening, how it is worked). The last
column says where aboard it belongs. Nothing here is built yet.
