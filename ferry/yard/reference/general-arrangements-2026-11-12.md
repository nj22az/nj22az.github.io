Johansson Shipyard · reference · 12 November 2026

anything from it ?"). **None of the files is in the repository.** Three are yards' or designers' own drawings and one is a
copyrighted book; a local copy of each is kept outside the repository, in `/Users/nilsjohansson/Developer/chisum-reference/`
on the owner's Mac (the drawings were read in the session's scratch folder and are fetched again from the addresses below).

**The rule for all of them is the repository's own (AGENTS.md, of REA and New Yokosuka): study and measure, never copy.**
What a real ship has, in what order, how large, and why, is knowledge. A drawing's lines, a book's text and its figures are
their owners'. Nothing is traced, and no text is taken over.

yard's own words.

## What was decided with them

Asked which ship she should become, the owner chose (12 November 2026): **a ro-ro freight ferry**, about 125 m, a classic
of the 1960s and 70s kept in service and refitted. The session's honest ranking of how surely each could be made extremely
real, given these sources: a multipurpose cargo ship like G4 (a complete real arrangement to follow, the fewest rooms), then
the ro-ro freight ferry (the guide has her midship section, doors, ramps, hoistable decks, lift and lashing; her
accommodation and engine room levels can be learned from G3 and G4; **what is missing is a real ro-ro's general
arrangement**, asked of the owner and searched for: `roro-freight-ships.md`), then the bulk carrier, then a passenger ferry.
