# The owner's ship drawings, read deck by deck

Read on 12 November 2026 by a research agent. The drawings belong to their yards and designers and are NOT in the
repository: they are in `/Users/nilsjohansson/Developer/chisum-reference/` on the owner's Mac. They are studied and
measured, never copied or traced. "n/r" means not readable in the drawing (or not printed on it). Names in "quotes" are
as printed, spelling mistakes included. Sizes marked "about" were scaled by the method stated at the head of each
section; trust them to roughly 0.2 m, not better.

| | File | What it turned out to be | Use for the 125 m ro-ro freight ferry |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | `GA-ferry-roro-rev1.pdf` | General arrangement of an unnamed 106.43 m ro-ro passenger and vehicle ferry, scale 1:250, vector CAD | High: vehicle decks, ramps, side door, tank grid, deck stack |

| C | `PSV_092Loa_Havila_Jupiter_GA.pdf` | Low-resolution raster of an anchor handler lettered "HAVILA VENUS", eight decks | Medium: deck heights, machinery and tank logic; room names mostly n/r |

Not one of the six shows a helideck, lifeboats in davits, a cargo lift, or drivers' cabins. Those four parts of the
125 m ship need other sources.
