Chisum

A 125 m ro-ro freight ferry, taken apart deck by deck. Drag to turn her, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to look closer, tap a room or a fitting to name it, or walk aboard.

W A S D or the arrow keys to walk, drag to look round, click a fitting to see what it is, Shift to go faster, the wheel or + and - to zoom (0 for the normal view), F at a ladder, Q and E for the deck below and above, 1 to 5 for a deck, M for the deck plan, Esc to leave.Left thumb to walk, drag anywhere else to look round, two fingers to zoom in and out, tap a fitting to see what it is, tap the plan for the whole deck. At a ladder a button takes it.
