# Tools for looking

Small helpers used while building her; none is part of the page or of the build's gate.

| File | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| `still.py` | A Cycles still of the built ship from a place given in the ship's terms (metres from the transom, to port, above the keel) | `blender -b <built.blend> -P ferry/tools/still.py -- --out x.png --eye 51.2,5.1,14.0 --aim 52.0,5.9,13.6 [--lens 35] [--samples 32] [--size 1300x900] [--lamp yard,x,h --watts 800]` (inside a room it needs `--lamp`: no light gets in otherwise) |
| `onfoot-wing.cjs` | The page itself, on foot on the port bridge wing, as an upright tablet shows it; opens the hose box | `node ferry/tools/onfoot-wing.cjs /tmp/out` (site served on port 8765) |
| `onfoot-pantry.cjs` | The page on foot at the pantry, its doors shut and open | `node ferry/tools/onfoot-pantry.cjs /tmp/out` |
| `card.cjs`, `card_crop.py` | The homepage card's picture: her whole length from the bow quarter, the page's chrome hidden, drawn on the machine's own graphics card when Chromium can reach it (it says which it got) | `node ferry/tools/card.cjs shot.png`, then `python3 ferry/tools/card_crop.py shot.png assets/images/ferry/chisum-card.webp` |
| `shots.cjs` | **The same thirty-two views of her every time** (six from outside: bow quarter, stern quarter, low on the beam, a true profile and a true plan through a long lens, in dock; twenty-six on foot: seven on the bridge in its own measure, and nineteen places in her rooms and on her decks, each given as a share of a room's box), from the page itself on the machine's own graphics card: for putting one step beside the last (the audit after every step). No place in it is written in metres of one ship: the file says where her bridge stands and where each room is | `node ferry/tools/shots.cjs /path/to/folder [only,these,views]`; `CHISUM_URL=http://localhost:8766/ferry/` for another copy of the site |
| `shots-stern.cjs` | **The same twenty-eight views of the stern ramp and what works it every time** (from astern raised, level and at its lowest; from inside; the cylinder, the hinges, the flaps, the cleats, the control stand, at rest and working, and from arm's length, where its legends and scales are read; the mooring station, the hydraulic pump room and its gauges from close, and the marked places in the main switchboard room and the auxiliary engine room), each an eye and an aim in the ship's own measures (metres to port, from the transom, above the keel), with the ramp put where the view says: for the audit before and after each stage of JS3-304 | `node ferry/tools/shots-stern.cjs /path/to/folder [only,these,views]` |
| `shots-cabin.cjs` | **The same eleven views of the chief engineer's day room every time**, and the page with the maintenance program open on its computer (the room, from the door, at the desk, the screen and the board's sign from arm's length, the desk's top, behind the screen, under the desk, the after wall, the calendar, the window) | `node ferry/tools/shots-cabin.cjs /path/to/folder [only,views]` |
| `look.py` | A quick look at the built ship from Blender itself, without the page: flat-lit workbench pictures, in seconds, for seeing a hull's form or a mistake in the steel before the whole make is run | `blender -b <built.blend> -P ferry/tools/look.py -- --out folder --views profile,plan,bow,stern,body,under,aft_under` or `--eye yard,x,height --aim yard,x,height` |
| `svgshot.cjs` | A picture of a yard drawing (SVG), whole or a part at twice the size | `node ferry/tools/svgshot.cjs sheet.svg out.png [x y w h]` |

The `onfoot` and `svgshot` helpers start Chromium with software drawing, as the page checks do: they prove a thing is there and works, not how it
looks on a real graphics card.
