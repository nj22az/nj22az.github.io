# Illustration envelope review A

Four generic equipment types. Millimetre envelopes are declared visual budgets, not real measurements. Pump: shared base aligns motor, guarded coupling and pump shaft; both flanges remain exposed. Compressor: receiver supported above feet, motor/pump on mounting deck, drain/gauge/outlet visible. Generator: common skid, enclosed alternator/coupling and side access to controls; exhaust is illustrative. Probe: readout, connector, flexible lead and probe distinguish the assembly. No building, installation, clearance or pressure-design approval is represented.

The exporter records actual mesh bounds and triangle totals for checking against these envelopes. Models contain no inferred PS, TS, volume, fluid group, category, inspection interval or legal decision. Review actual Blender renders before delivery.

Final render review: pump motor sole and coupling-guard brackets meet the common skid; compressor cylinders have a supporting crankcase and gauge/inlet connections have visible mounting bosses; readout and probe are posed against the display plane.
