## Owner decision - 23 October 2026: discreet comic authorship

New comics made for Mr Johansson include the small editable "Johansson" creator wordmark by default, near the final panel's lower corner. This is an artist wordmark, not a claimed reproduction of a handwritten signature. Keep it subtle and clear of dialogue/captions. Honour a saved or explicitly requested choice to change or remove it.

Use Studio's `creatorMark` project setting and final comic compositor. Do not bake the mark into clean picture/depth layers, stamp every panel, put it over gameplay, or silently add it to legacy imported projects without the setting. Preserve it through project export/reopen and editable sharing. For signed exports from legacy projects, explicitly enable the setting. The owner may supply a particular signature or logo later; do not invent an authentic signature on their behalf.
