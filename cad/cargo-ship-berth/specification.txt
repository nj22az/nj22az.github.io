COASTAL CARGO SHIP AND BERTH — CONCEPT A
25 October 2026

Purpose
An original exterior CAD concept for a believable coastal general cargo vessel
and its berth. Created from scratch; no geometry from the existing game model
is used. The reference screenshots supply context, not measured dimensions.

Arrangement
Bow points toward +X. The quay is on the negative-Y, starboard side.
Hull station sections form a broad working midbody, rounded chine transitions,
a narrowing stern and a fine bow. Plates have navy topsides, a dark boot topping
around the waterline, and red underwater antifouling. Deck surfaces are green.
The hull is an exterior solid envelope; internal holds are not excavated.

Two hatch coamings, each 16.00 x 8.20, at X = -9.00 and +11.00. Eight separate
cover panels per hatch, each 1.96 x 8.22. Panel tops at z = +3.64. Nominal deck
width outside each coaming is about 2.65 per side in the parallel midbody.

Aft accommodation has lower and upper levels, an enclosed wheelhouse,
individual dark glazing, projecting bridge wings, navigation light housings,
funnel, radar scanner, antennas and mainmast. Twin small pedestal cranes are
positioned on the port side at X = -19.00 and +1.00, Y = +4.90. Their booms are
stowed mainly fore-and-aft. No crane lifting capacity is specified.

Deck fittings include railings, forecastle access treads, anchor windlasses,
schematic chains and anchors, mooring bitts, vents, enclosed rescue boat,
davits and two liferaft capsules. These are external visual components.
Propulsion, rudder, machinery, internal stairs and working mechanisms are
outside this first exterior arrangement.

The independent quay model includes a slab, fascia beam, three rows of
supporting piles, transverse cap beams, coping, yellow edge stripe,
movement-joint lines, rubber fender elements, face panels, horn bollards,
three access ladders and two lighting poles. The combined assembly adds
four schematic mooring lines and the access gangway. Mooring lines are
for visual arrangement; lead angles, fairleads and loads need further design.
Pile embedment, water depth, seabed and land connection are not determined.

Design status
Conceptual visual CAD, with assumed dimensions. No displacement, deadweight,
stability, structural scantlings, berth capacity, fender energy, mooring loads
or regulatory compliance have been calculated. The separate CAD solids pass
geometric validity checks; this does not establish a buildable vessel or quay.
The drawing is schematic; editable CAD geometry controls.
