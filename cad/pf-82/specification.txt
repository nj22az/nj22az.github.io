JOHANSSON PF-82 — POLIVOKS FILTER SYNTHESISER — CAD REVISION A
3 November 2026

Purpose
The PF-82 as a physical instrument: a Johansson Denki desktop synthesiser of
about 1982, designed for the web simulator at /polivoks/ and drawn from
scratch. Its panel carries exactly the controls of the page, in the page's
order, so the three.js model (polivoks/view3d.js) can turn the same knobs.
No geometry, artwork or dimensions are taken from the Formanta Polivoks or
from clarionut's Eurorack module.

Files

Overall (millimetres)
Width over the cheeks:            640
Depth:                             400
Height at the rear of the cheeks:  152
Case between the cheeks:           600 wide, extruded side section
Cheeks:                            2 × 20 thick, walnut
Feet:                              4 × Ø24 × 6, rubber

Keyboard
37 keys: three octaves and a top C (C2 to C5 at the default octave).
White keys:   pitch 23.5, body 22.5 wide, 150 long, 20 high, top at z = 60.
Black keys:   13 wide, 95 long, 12 proud of the white keys.
Key frame:    top at z = 40; key fronts 14 behind the case front; key slip 10 deep.
Travel:       4 (the page moves a key down 4 when it sounds).

Panel
Brushed black aluminium, 2 thick, 600 wide, 219 along the slope.
Front edge at y = 185, z = 78; rear edge at y = 395, z = 140; slope 16.45°.
Panel co-ordinates: u across from the left, v up the slope from the front edge.
Header strip v 195–219: wordmark, lamp at u 548, power rocker at u 574.
Three columns (u 4–198, 202–398, 402–596) and three rows
(v 130–192, 66–128, 2–64), numbered as on the page's signal-flow map:
  1 VCO 1       2 VCO 2        4 VCF (rows 1 and 2, right column)
  3 MIXER       6 ENV 1
  5 VCA + ENV 2 7 LFO          8 KEYBOARD

Controls
Knobs:        Ø20 cap (Ø25 skirt), 15 high, 1.2 chamfer, cream pointer.
Filter knobs: FREQUENCY and RESONANCE Ø30 cap, 18 high, at v = 151.
Selectors:    chicken-head, Ø14 boss, 24 × 6 bar; at the middle position
              they point up the slope; 2 to 5 positions over ±35° to ±70°.
Jacks:        6 on the filter, v = 80, 32 apart: CV 1, CV 2, AUDIO IN,
              LP OUT, BP OUT, MIX OUT (the lit output feeds the VCA).
Knob law:     0 at 7 o'clock, 10 at 5 o'clock, 270° clockwise, as on screen.

layout, in English or Russian, with the same words as the on-screen panel.
